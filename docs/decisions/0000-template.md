# NNNN — Short imperative title

Date: YYYY-MM-DD
Status: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN

## Context

What forces are at play. What made this a decision rather than an obvious call.
State facts, and where a fact came from — measured, read in the source, or
assumed.

## Decision

What was chosen, stated plainly and in the active voice.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what this commits us to. Include
the costs honestly — an ADR that lists only upsides is marketing.

## Alternatives rejected

Mandatory. For each: what it was, and the specific reason it lost. This section
is the point of the document — the next agent needs to know which roads have
already been walked, so it does not re-propose them.
